The long-term direction

Our ambition

Ltopic is exploring a multi-model cognitive architecture that connects an organisation’s documents, business rules, observations and tools.

✧  Business knowledge
✧  Traceability
✧  Multi-model orchestration
✧  Data control

The software platform is a project under development, separate from the consulting services currently offered.

What we want to make possible

A company’s useful knowledge extends beyond its documents. It also includes decisions, constraints, methods and its teams’ experience. Our ambition is to connect these elements so that they remain understandable and reusable over time.

Memory that preserves context

Link information to the project, decision and conditions that give it meaning, rather than treating it in isolation.

Answers that can be examined

Make sources and uncertainties visible so teams can check the information being used.

Multiple models and suitable tools

Use different capabilities according to business needs, without reducing the entire architecture to a single AI model.

Human and organisational control

Design access rights, approvals and deployment arrangements around each organisation’s responsibilities and constraints.
