Privacy and cookies

Your enquiries

Ltopic, represented by Bruno Pereira, uses the contact form information to assess and respond to your enquiry, for pre-contractual steps or its legitimate interest in managing professional correspondence. Name, job title, company, email and description of your needs are required; phone is optional. Do not include sensitive information in the message.

Delivery and retention

FormSubmit is the planned service for delivering messages to Ltopic’s mailbox. Sending remains disabled until activation and verification. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions in its archive for 30 days. Ltopic plans to delete copies of enquiries that do not proceed within 12 months of the last exchange, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Audience measurement

Google Analytics 4 is planned to measure visits, traffic sources and commercial interactions only with permission. Microsoft Clarity is planned to analyse browsing through heatmaps and session recordings, with separate permission. These services remain inactive until their configuration is complete. Clarity is not loaded on Contact. Names, contact details and messages are never included in analytics events.

Your choices

You will be able to accept all, reject all or choose by purpose. Refusing measurement does not block the form when it is available. Your choice is stored on your device for up to six months; Cookie settings lets you change it. Withdrawal does not automatically erase previously collected data.

Recipients and safeguards

Only Ltopic and providers required for each purpose receive the relevant data. Google and Microsoft services may involve processing outside the European Union. Processing terms and transfer safeguards must be reviewed within the accounts before activation. Advertising use is not planned.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing under the applicable conditions. You may also complain to the French CNIL. Use Contact when it is available and indicate that your enquiry concerns your personal data.
