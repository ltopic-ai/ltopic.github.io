Privacy and cookies

Controller and contact

Ltopic, represented by Bruno Pereira, controls this website’s audience measurement. For privacy questions or requests: bruno@ltopic.ai.

Measurement with your consent

Google Analytics 4 measures visits, pages, traffic sources, device categories and interactions with commercial links. Consent is the legal basis. No Analytics script loads before you accept. Refusing does not prevent browsing.

Data and recipients

Ltopic can access the data, which Google processes to provide Analytics. Data includes pseudonymous browser identifiers, device characteristics and navigation events. Names, email addresses, phone numbers and form text are not sent in our Analytics events. Query strings and fragments are excluded from transmitted page addresses. Our tag disables advertising personalisation and Google Signals.

Cookies and retention

Your consent choice is stored locally for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies starting with _ga are limited to 180 days without automatic renewal on each visit. Analytics data retention depends on the property settings; aggregated reports may outlast individual events.

Withdrawal and rights

Use “Manage cookies” in the footer to refuse or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal stops new measurements and removes Analytics cookies accessible to the site; it does not undo previous processing. Contact the address above to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable. You can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority: CNIL.

Provider and transfers

Google may process data in different countries, including outside the European Union. Read its information on partner sites and transfer safeguards. GitHub Pages hosting may also process technical data needed to deliver and secure this site.

Contact form and other tools

The commercial contact form is currently disabled and cannot send enquiries. Microsoft Clarity is not active. This page will be updated before additional processing is introduced.
