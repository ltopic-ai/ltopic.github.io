From the first conversation to the findings

How does an engagement work?

1. Define the need

We clarify the problem to examine, the teams involved, constraints and the expected outcome. Scope, deliverables and terms are agreed before we begin.

2. Observe and map

We talk with teams, observe activities and review relevant documents and tools to understand how work actually happens.

3. Assess potential uses

We assess observed difficulties against AI capabilities, taking available data, feasibility and required changes into account.

4. Present findings and next steps

We present priorities and next steps. If deployment is being considered, we clarify the requirements to share with technical partners.

What you receive

Deliverables to help you decide and move forward

Depending on the agreed scope, the findings include the following deliverables.

  1. A map of processes and informationSteps, stakeholders, tools and key information flows.
  2. An analysis of observed difficultiesFriction points and their operational consequences.
  3. Prioritised AI use casesA reasoned selection based on business value, feasibility and identified limitations.
  4. Prerequisites to addressData, tools, constraints and the expected involvement of your teams.
  5. A roadmapNext steps and requirements to share with an integrator or technical team.

The exact scope, duration and price are agreed after scoping. Deployment support is subject to a separate agreed scope.
